## If something looks wrong

- **The "Git" lines of the checkpoint tool look stale in the cloud workspace** (an old commit, many "changed" files): that copy has its own old `.git`. Ignore it there; the real history is `.git/logs/HEAD` in Jeremy's folder.
- **Checkpoint reports "CHANGED":** a tracked file differs from the last checkpoint. If Jeremy edited it on purpose, accept it and record it in the session log; if not, find out before building on it.
- **Checkpoint reports the sprite or an image:** saving through the bridge adds an invisible tag to images and to `tools/pages/_sprite.html`. The tool and the builder already ignore it.
- **A script's scrolling is undone, or full-page screenshots show blank photo frames:** the pages hold themselves at the top until a visitor touches, scrolls or presses a key (or 8 seconds pass), and the photos use native lazy loading. A script must press a key first, then scroll in steps and wait (`tools/shots.py` does).
- **The Contact page's FAQ is chosen by exact question text** (`FAQ["contact"]` in `tools/build-pages.py`): "Do I have to sell to you if I reach out?", "How do I know GLP is legit?" and "What areas do you serve?". If a later session rewords one of those three, it silently drops off the Contact page; change the filter at the same time.
- **The contact form looks wrong, shows the Call, Text and Email card, or the green button does nothing (Checkpoint 46):** the form needs `js/form.js` (deferred, on Home and Contact) and `js/site.js`. If either has not arrived after 6 seconds, or crashes, the head script removes the `js` class and the page shows the Call, Text and Email card instead; if the scripts arrive later the form comes back by itself (`python3 tools/safety-test.py` plays this). `python3 tools/form-test.py` replays every state in both modes against a made-up receiver; it never types into a real form. With `FORM_ENDPOINT` empty the green button builds a text and email for the visitor's own app (`sms:` and `mailto:` links); a computer without a text app may do nothing on the text button, which is why computers and tablets get the email button first and every panel shows the number and a Copy button. The AI cannot test real phone apps.
- **(Gone at Checkpoint 46)** The FORM-TRIM rule for the old GoHighLevel frame no longer exists; the form is the site's own, and its look is the `lf-` rules in `css/site.css`.
- **A wrong address shows GitHub's own plain "404" instead of ours (Checkpoint 36):** GitHub Pages is documented to serve the repo's root `404.html` for any missing address, and our page's links start with the site folder (`SITE` in `tools/build-pages.py` sets that) so they work from any depth, but this was not proven on a project address before the push. Open `https://jsglisson3.github.io/GLP-Home-Options/nope` and write down what shows. If it is GitHub's plain page, nothing else is broken (a wrong address leads nowhere either way); wait a few minutes (Pages can take a short while to pick up a new file) before changing anything. `tools/notfound-test.py` proves the page itself works when it is shown.
- **"Print my numbers" does nothing on someone's phone:** the page calls the browser's own print command; a browser that blocks it (some in-app browsers, such as a link opened inside another app) will ignore it. Opening the page in Safari or Chrome works. Printing the whole page with the browser's own Print still prints the whole page, as before.
- **The AI's browser tab is hidden:** `requestAnimationFrame` never fires and computed styles can be stale mid-transition, so check with screenshots and synchronous scripts, not timers.
- **Facebook:** our tools cannot fetch it (it refuses them) and we never sign in. Ask Joey or Jeremy for saved photos or video files.
- **Our work photo groups:** photos of mixed shapes flow into columns; a group whose photos are all the same shape sits in rows (the builder decides). Since Checkpoint 48 no photo sits alone in a last row: with two across an odd one out spans the row, with three across a leftover one makes the last row four photos and a leftover two share it (`.work-rows` in `css/site.css`).
