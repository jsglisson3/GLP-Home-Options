Questions

Straight answers.

Still wondering about something? Ask us directly. There's no such thing as a silly question when it's your home.

Call or text (423) 451-5707 Email info@glphomeoptions.com
Do I have to sell to you if I reach out?

No. There's no obligation. We'll show you every realistic option, including listing with an agent or keeping the home, and you decide what's right.

How do I know GLP is legit?

Fair question. You deal directly with Joey or Ethan, and you can reach us at (423) 451-5707 or info@glphomeoptions.com. You can see our recent closing and the home we're building on our Facebook page (opens in a new tab), and you're always free to have your own attorney or title company review anything before you sign.

How fast can you close if I sell directly?

As little as 7 days, and usually 7 to 14. You pick the closing date, so if you need more time to move, that works too.

Do I need to fix anything or clean out the house?

Not if you sell directly to us. We buy as-is, with no repairs, no cleaning, and no showings. Just one visit so we can see the home.

Are there fees or commissions when I sell to you?

No commissions and no fees, and we pay the closing costs on a direct sale.

Why would I list instead of taking a cash offer?

If your home is move-in ready and you aren't in a hurry, listing on the open market usually nets the most money. We'll tell you plainly if that's your best move.

What if I want to keep my home but I'm struggling?

We can help you think through refinancing, renting the property out, or other ways to get past short-term financial trouble so you can stay.

What if my situation doesn't fit a standard path?

Tell us what's going on. For unique situations where the standard paths don't feel like a fit, we explore structured options together. See how it works.

Can you help me figure out where to go next?

Often, yes. In a recent sale the owners needed a place to move, so we worked around their timeline and helped them find a rental.

What areas do you serve?

We work in Chattanooga, TN, and we're based in Hixson. Not sure whether your address is in our area? Call or text us and we'll tell you.

Do you build homes too?

Yes. We build affordable new homes in Chattanooga for first-time buyers and families. Ask us what's coming up next. See what we're building.
