Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us. Last updated October 21, 2026.

This policy explains what GLP Home Options (operated by GL Partners LLC, “we” or “us”) collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain on purpose.

1. What information we collect

We collect the personal information you choose to give us when you ask about our services, for example when you fill out the contact form, book a call, call or text us, or email us. That usually means your name, email address, phone number, and details about your property.

2. How we use your information

We use what you share to communicate with you, to understand your situation, and to offer options that fit your property. We may reach out by email, phone call, or text message (SMS).

3. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us run this website and our business (for example, the software that hosts our contact form, scheduling, and messaging). They agree to keep your information confidential.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to make browsing easier, to understand how visitors use the site, and to learn what people find helpful. Cookies are small files stored on your device that remember your preferences and recognize returning visitors.

You can control or turn off cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work properly without them. We respect “Do Not Track” signals when your browser sends them.

5. Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in, we use your phone number to text you about your inquiry. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Message and data rates may apply.

6. Security

We use a variety of security measures to protect your personal information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.

9. Contact us

Questions or concerns about this policy? Email info@glphomeoptions.com. Mailing address: GLP Home Options (Legal entity: GL Partners LLC), PO Box 55, Hixson, TN.
