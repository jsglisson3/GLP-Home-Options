# Joey's kit: texts and questions you can send as they are

_Drafted Oct 22, 2026 (Session 6g). Nothing here is on the site and nothing here is sent. Every message is a draft for Joey to change into his own voice, and he should send them from his own phone. The review rules come from the FTC and Google pages summarized in `compliance-questions-for-joey.md` (research for the family, not legal advice)._

## 1. Asking past sellers for a review (the safe way)

**The rules in one breath.** The same short message goes to every past seller, not just the ones Joey expects to be happy. No gift, discount, entry or favor for writing one. Joey never writes, edits or suggests the words. No asking for a particular star rating, a particular topic, or a staff name. Joey, Ethan and their relatives do not post reviews. One link (or a QR code) to Google, and nothing else. Details and sources: `compliance-questions-for-joey.md`, section "Reviews".

**Text to send** (change anything you like, keep it neutral):

> Hi [first name], it's Joey from GLP Home Options. Thank you again for letting us help with your house. If you have a minute, an honest review on Google helps other homeowners in Chattanooga decide who to trust. Here's the link: [Google review link]. Say whatever is true for you, good or bad, and no reply is needed. Either way, I'm glad we met.

**If someone replies with a complaint:** thank them, say you will call, and call. Do not ask them to delete or change anything and do not answer in a way that argues.

**Before the first one goes out:** Joey needs the real Google review link (from his Google Business Profile) and a list of past sellers who are fine being texted. If he is not sure someone is fine being texted, leave them off the list.

## 2. Asking a seller if we may use their story or photo

Tennessee law (as one secondary source describes it) bars using a person's name or likeness in advertising without their consent, so we want a yes in writing (a text reply that says yes counts as a written record; keep a screenshot).

> Hi [first name], it's Joey. We are building the new GLP website and I'd love to share a short version of your story, with only your first name and your neighborhood, and the photo from closing day if you're comfortable with it. You can say no, or ask to see it first, and it won't change anything between us. Would that be okay?

Keep the answer, the date and exactly what they agreed to (story, first name, neighborhood, photo) together in one place. Anything they said no to stays off the site. The site shows only what Joey confirms, so nothing from this goes live until Joey says so.

## 3. Your story, in your own words (for the About page)

Ten minutes each, Joey and Ethan separately, by voice memo or text. We would quote you, not write for you. Please skip anything you would not say out loud to a neighbor.

1. How did you two start doing this, and what was the first house?
2. Why did you want to work with homeowners who are short on time or money?
3. What do people say when they first call? What are they worried about?
4. What do you tell someone who is nervous about calling a company like ours?
5. What is one thing you will not do, even if it would make you money?
6. Tell me about a closing you were proud of (no names or addresses needed).
7. What does a good first conversation sound like? What do you ask?
8. What do you wish more homeowners knew before they sold?
9. What are you building next, and why that?
10. What should a homeowner expect from you in the first week?

## 4. If a seller says yes to a testimonial (not a review)

A testimonial goes on our own site, so the FTC treats it as our advertising. That means: only real customers, only their real words, their written yes, and a result that is typical for what you do (or a plain note of what is typical). If the story is a fast close, say "a typical sale takes X" only when Joey can show it from his own closing records. If a seller got anything for sharing it, we say so right next to it.

Three questions to ask them, same for everyone:

1. How did you first hear about GLP?
2. What was your situation, in your own words?
3. How did it go, and how would you describe working with Joey and Ethan?

We use their answers as written, trimmed only for length, and we show them the final text before it goes up.

## 5. What this kit does not cover

- The five questions Joey needs to answer first (photos, closing story, claims, license, phone) are in `questions-for-joey.md`. Send those before anything here.
- Texting a review request to someone is itself a text from a business. Until Joey tells us how his number and any registered texting campaign are set up (see `lead-response-playbook.md`), send these one to one from his personal phone, one person at a time, to people he already knows, and ask him to check the texting rules in `compliance-questions-for-joey.md` first.
