## The short version

1. **A PO box cannot be the profile's address.** Google's pages call PO boxes and remote mailboxes unacceptable, and its policy overview says a profile set up at a PO box "will have their profile suspended". The site today lists `PO Box 55, Hixson, TN`. Everything below follows from that.
2. **The sanctioned shape for a business with no staffed office is a "service-area business":** a real place Google can find (used for verification; practitioners believe it also anchors the map position, but Google does not say so), **hidden from the public**, plus up to 20 service areas within about two hours' drive. The real place is a decision only Joey can make (section 1).
3. **No Google page mentions "we buy houses".** Eligibility has to be read from the general rules (in-person contact with customers, no profiles for properties for sale, no "lead generation" companies). Nothing found says GLP is ineligible, and nothing says it is welcome. Present GL Partners as the business that buys, renovates and builds itself.
4. **I found no "house buyer" category** (not on the third-party category list I checked; Google's own list is only visible in the profile's picker, so type it in to see what Google offers). Every choice is a compromise, and the primary category is the top-scored ranking factor in the expert survey. Pick it deliberately (section 2).
5. **According to one agency (Sterling Sky, Sept 2026), the top reasons it sees for suspension are:** too many edits at once, no physical presence at the address (PO boxes, virtual offices, coworking), a suspended manager account, duplicate listings, and "spammy" industries. Fill in everything correctly once, then change one thing at a time.
6. **Do this after the site is live on its final address,** with the same name, phone and wording. Do not use the github.io address as the website.

## 1. The address (the one that decides everything)

| Option | What the sources say | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **PO box or remote mailbox** | Google: not acceptable; PO-box profiles "will have their profile suspended". Sterling Sky's list of top suspension reasons includes "Your business doesn't have a physical presence at the address you used. (E.g. PO box, virtual office, coworking space, etc)". | Highest. Never put the PO box anywhere in the profile or the verification. It can still be used for postal mail outside Google. |
| **Virtual office or rented mailing address** | Google: not eligible where the business does not operate; a service-area business may list a virtual office only if it is staffed during business hours. Sterling Sky lists virtual offices as a hard-suspension trigger. | High. |
| **Co-working space** | Allowed only with clear signage, customers received during hours, and the business's own staff present (Google). | Medium to high unless GLP really works there at posted hours. |
| **A founder's home, hidden (service-area business)** | Google's own example is a plumber working from home who should hide the address. A forum moderator says a home office can be verified by showing the home address, desk, computer and equipment, and that signage is not required for a service-area business. | Medium. Depends on passing the verification (below). |
| **A real staffed office with signage, address shown** | Google: show an address only if staffed and open to customers at posted hours with permanent signage. | Lowest rule risk; highest cost and obligation. |

- **Practitioners believe a hidden address still anchors the map position** (Google does not say so). The expert survey scores "proximity of address to the point of search" second among map factors, and the survey's author writes that he is unconvinced showing versus hiding the address is a factor at all. A Hixson base is strongest in north Chattanooga; searchers elsewhere in the area will favor businesses closer to them.
- **Hiding is about what the public sees.** Google's pages say to enter an address "Google can find" to verify, and that a hidden address means the public profile "will only show your service area". (My reading: Google still holds the address, and it becomes the verification place.)
- **The sources disagree on where the verification video must be shot.** Google's help page says to be "at your business"; one forum moderator says the location should match the "service area you entered" (his reading of a failure notice that was posted as an image, so Google's own wording could not be read). Which applies to a hidden home address is unresolved: follow the on-screen prompt on the day.
- **The site has to agree.** Today the footer, Contact page and the search data say `PO Box 55, Hixson, TN` (launch-checklist decision 3). If the profile hides the address, decide what the site shows (for example "Based in Hixson, TN" with no street address) and change both together.

## 2. Categories (only the live picker is authoritative)

Google says: "use as few categories as possible to describe your overall core business", "as specific as possible, but representative of your main business", and a category should complete "This business IS a…", not "HAS a…". Do not use categories as keywords.

Two 2026 third-party lists of Google's categories show **no** category for a house buyer, cash buyer or real estate investor. Both lists are vendor pages that do not name their data source, they disagree with each other, and Google changes categories without announcing it, so **type each word into the profile's own category picker and read what Google offers before choosing.** Names found on the third-party lists, by what GLP does:

| GLP activity | Names found (verify in the picker) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buying houses from homeowners | Nothing that says "home buyer". Nearest: **Property investment company**; also "Investment company". | Its fit with people searching "sell my house" is unknown. Do not use "Foreclosure service" or "Real estate developer" unless the picker explains them and they are true. |
| Renovating | Remodeler; General contractor; Building restoration service; Home staging service | Honest for the renovation side only. |
| Building new homes | Home builder; Custom home builder; Construction company | Honest as primary only if building is a large share of the work. |
| Brokerage-type | Real estate agency; Real estate agent; Real estate consultant | **Most likely to misrepresent a business that buys for its own account.** Google's Business Profile policy says merchants "must accurately represent their business identity" and treats edits that "significantly change the nature of a business" as often fraudulent. No Google text ties them to a license, and the licensing question is for an attorney or the Tennessee Real Estate Commission. |

- **A mixed set follows Google's rule:** one primary that is honestly what GLP is most of the time, plus a few accurate secondaries. The owners choose which activity is the main business. (Google's "Edit your Business Profile" page says one primary category plus "up to 9 more".)
- **Expect limited map visibility for "we buy houses" searches.** The category and the name are the strongest signals, the right category is missing, and the name cannot carry keywords. The website and searches for "GLP Home Options" carry more of the load. This is reasoning from an expert survey, not a measured result.
- **Competitors' categories could not be found with the tools available.** Anyone can see them: search each competitor on Google Maps and read the category under its name (the private report lists whom to look at).
- **ATTORNEY:** Google excludes "lead generation agents or companies" and does not define it. If GLP ever passes contracts on to other buyers, ask how that fits before the profile mentions it.

## 4. Hours: a real decision with no clean answer

- Google: "Certain types of businesses shouldn't provide hours, including those with varied hours [...] and those that operate only by appointment."
- Expert survey (opinion): "business open at time of search" is ranked 5th among map factors and "proper hours set" 21st, up from 85th in 2023.
- Google's live video verification is "only available within the business hours of your location", so a profile with no hours may not be offered that method (unconfirmed).
- The site says "you can reach us anytime". **Claiming 24/7 hours would misstate the business.** The choice is between (a) real hours when Joey or Ethan will actually answer or call back, or (b) none. Decide on how customers really reach them, then keep the site's wording consistent.

## 5. Before you create anything (the evidence kit)

Gather these first. Google's appeals tool can be used **only once** and its processing time is not published, so the proof should exist before anything goes wrong.

- [ ] The site live on its final address, indexable, with no redirect chain (launch checklist).
- [ ] **A search on Google Maps** for "GLP Home Options" and the phone number, to see whether a listing already exists (claim it instead of making a second; Google may suspend an account for "multiple profiles for the same business in the same metropolitan area").
- [ ] A **domain email** (for example info@glphomeoptions.com) for the primary owner account, not a personal Gmail; one management account only. Do not make Google Maps edits from the account that manages the profile.
- [ ] The Search Console property for the domain (Google's verification page says verification can sometimes be instant through Search Console).
- [ ] **Registration papers** for GL Partners LLC, and anything linking the brand "GLP Home Options" to it (an assumed-name filing, if one exists: **ACCOUNTANT/ATTORNEY**), a license or tax certificate if one applies, and a **utility bill or similar proof of the operating address**. Google tells appealing owners to make sure "the business name and address match the profile".
- [ ] Photos of branded materials (a card, a sign, a vehicle) and a short walkthrough video of the base and tools.
- [ ] **For a video verification** (the likely method for a service-area business with no storefront). Google's page says to be at your business and show: your current location (outside signs, nearby businesses or a street sign), your business or equipment (products, branded equipment, marketing materials or tools), and proof you manage the business (access to employee-only areas or items like a cash register, kitchen, storage room or non-sensitive business documents; your keys to open a facility also work). A Local Search Forum moderator adds, as forum advice and not Google's wording: unlock the front door of a home office before entering, you could log into a computer, show the business name printed on a card, a vehicle or a license, and do not show faces. His takeaway from a failed-verification notice (posted as an image, so Google's wording could not be read) is that the location should match the service area entered. Follow the on-screen prompt on the day.

## 6. The sequence (my synthesis of the sources; no one publishes a 90-day plan)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Before day 0** | Website live on the final address. Name, one main phone, address choice, service areas and categories settled and written down. Evidence kit ready. |
| **Day 0: create once** | Choose the service-area option, enter the real base and hide it, add the service areas, and enter the final name, phone, website and categories **in the first submission**. No keywords, no "LLC", no links, no prices. Do not fill in everything else in the same sitting if a step is unclear; it is better to be complete and accurate than fast. |
| **Days 1 to 14: verification** | Complete the method Google offers (video, or a postcard if one is offered). Change nothing in the name, address or category. **Do not request a second code** (it invalidates the first). Postcards usually arrive within 14 days and expire after 30; reviews take up to 5 business days. If "Get verified" reappears, Google could not finish: answer what it asks. |
| **Days 15 to 30: stabilize** | One change at a time, spaced several days apart: services, description, photos, attributes. Wait until each shows (Google: edits "usually take up to 10 minutes to review, but sometimes it can take up to 30 days") before the next. |
| **Days 30 to 60: content and reviews** | Add real photos and a short video. Start asking real customers for reviews with the profile's own link, and reply to each (`research/review-kit.md`). No keyword-stuffed posts. |
| **Days 60 to 90: review** | Look at the profile's calls and website clicks. Correct inaccuracies one at a time. Decide whether to revisit hours or test a tracking number. |
| **Always** | One profile only (never one per founder or per town). Website, Facebook and profile agree on name and phone. |

**Planning figures:** most postcards arrive within 14 days (Google gives no maximum, and a code expires after 30 days); Google's review step takes up to 5 business days after a code or a video. If a profile is suspended, the appeals tool is the route; practitioners report about a week to six weeks, with no 2026 figure.

## 7. If the profile is suspended

- Use Google's appeals tool, signed in to the related account; attach the evidence kit; it can be submitted **only once**, though a contact form may allow an additional review with evidence not sent the first time.
- **Do not create a new profile for the same business.** Google says so plainly; one practitioner says only as a last resort. A second profile risks the duplicate cause and an account-level penalty.
- A "soft" suspension (the profile still shows but the owner has lost control) and a "hard" one (the listing is removed) are practitioners' terms, not Google's.
- Add proof of the business to the profile while waiting: photos of materials, a vehicle, a walkthrough video.

## 8. What to expect

- Profile first appears after verification; customers can find it only after verification is complete.
- Google: "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google." Its factors are relevance, distance and prominence, and prominence depends on reviews and links around the web.
- The review habit is the lever Joey controls most; it is slow (about one review a month or fewer is a realistic assumption; see `research/review-kit.md`).
- No source gives a ranking or call forecast for a new profile at day 30, 60 or 90.
