## What's built so far (glp-home-options.html)
One-page site, single self-contained HTML file with all photos embedded:
- Header with their house logo, nav, and a phone button
- Hero: "Every path for your home, laid out clearly." with an interactive trail map ("You are here" with three paths)
- "We help with" situation chips
- Chattanooga riverfront photo band: "We live and work in Chattanooga, same as you."
- Three path cards plus a "creative solutions" strip
- Listing vs. selling direct comparison table
- Interactive seller net sheet (listing vs. cash offer, side by side)
- How it works (3 steps) beside the kitchen-table photo
- About: "Guides, not gurus," Joey and Ethan on the Walnut Street Bridge, headshots
- FAQ, contact section with lead form (preview only, not connected), footer with disclaimer

Design: deep pine green and river green with a trail-blaze gold accent, topographic contour lines in the background. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headings), Public Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (numbers and labels). Works in light and dark mode and on phones.
