# Photo brief for Joey

**For Jeremy:** photos are the single biggest upgrade left. Every photo we have is about 800 px wide, so it looks soft on big screens, and a few have unconfirmed origins. Send this to Joey with the short text in `questions-for-joey.md`. Nothing here has been sent. Real photos of real people and real houses are what makes the site believable, so this brief asks for the ordinary originals, not edited or AI-made images.

## What to send, in order of value

1. **A new portrait of you and Ethan together** (the hero photo)
   - **Upright (portrait orientation)**, from the waist up, standing side by side.
   - **Leave space**: about as much empty space above your heads as the height of a head, and some on each side. The website frames it in a house-shaped outline that trims the top corners.
   - **Good light**: outdoors in the hour before sunset or on an overcast day; not noon sun, not flash. Plain polo shirts like the current photo are great.
   - **Real places**: somewhere that says Chattanooga (the bridge again, the riverfront, in front of a house you worked on).
2. **One upright portrait of each of you** (same light, head and shoulders, space above).
3. **A real "talking it through" photo** at a kitchen table with a real homeowner who agrees to be in it (see "Permission" below).
4. **Houses**: for each recent home, the outside from the street (a clear day), a few inside shots, and a "before" if you have one. Progress photos of the new build are good too (the current ones are great).
5. **The original of the Walnut Street Bridge photo and the kitchen-table photo already on the site**, and a line saying who took them and when. If they are not real photos of you, say so; we will replace them.

## How to take and send them (this matters more than the camera)

- Use the **normal back camera** of a recent phone, at the highest quality setting. Not the selfie camera, not a screenshot, not a photo of a photo.
- Hold the phone **upright** for people shots. Keep it steady and tap on a face to focus.
- **Send the original files**: AirDrop, a shared Google Drive or iCloud folder, or email as attachments. **Do not send through text messages, WhatsApp, Messenger or Facebook**; they shrink the pictures and strip the details.
- Don't filter, retouch or crop them. We will crop for the website.
- Aim for **at least 1600 px on the long side** (any current phone does this), and send 5 to 10 of each shot so we can pick the best.

## Permission (before any photo with someone else in it)

- Get a **yes in writing** (a text or email is fine) from every homeowner or visitor who is recognizable in a photo or named in a story, saying where it may be used (the website, Facebook).
- Anything showing a **title company's or another business's sign or logo** needs their OK too, or crop it out.
- Don't show house numbers, license plates or documents with names.
- Because of licensing and advertising rules, **a lawyer should say what captions may claim** (for example "Just closed" or prices). We write neutral captions: "Closed September 2026", "Now building".

## What we will do with them

Hero and About portraits; the Home "how it works" photo; the gallery (Session 10); the New homes page. We keep your originals in the project's `research/photos/` folder and publish web copies sized for each screen. Until real photos are confirmed, the site stays hidden from search engines.
