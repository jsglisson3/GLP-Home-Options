## Round 2: what the Oct 22 review found we still cannot say (not sent)

The research on wary home sellers (`what-works-2026-10-22.md`) says the same few worries come up again and again: *Who is really buying? How do you come up with the price? What do I pay? Can I back out? Is this company real?* Our site answers some of them well (the net sheet, "bring your own attorney") but not the ones below. Each answer would become a short, plain box on the site, and we use only what is true on every deal. Items 13 to 15 and 21 are the biggest.

13. **How you set an offer, and how GLP earns.** In plain words, how do you come up with a price, and how does GLP make its money (resale after repairs, rental, building)? Roughly what share of a house's market value does a typical offer land at, in a normal house and in a house that needs a lot of work? Our net sheet's example uses 85% ($255,000 on $300,000); is that honest, or should the example be lower or shown as a range? What could change an offer after you see the house?
14. **What a seller pays at closing on a direct sale.** The site says "no fees" and "we pay the closing costs." Is that every cost (title and recording fees, transfer tax, the seller's share of property tax, any liens or payoff fees)? What exactly may we say? Which title company or attorney closes your deals, and is it always independent of GLP?
15. **The contract.** Do you buy with your own money and close in GLP's name, every time? Do you ever sell a signed contract to another buyer? How long does an offer stay open? What does your purchase agreement say if a seller changes their mind, and until when? May we show a sample (with made-up numbers) on the site?
16. **Your promises.** Which three to six promises do you keep on every deal? For example: no money from the seller before closing; the seller can show our offer to an attorney, an agent or family; no deadline to accept; we explain any change in writing before anything is signed. Only the ones you will keep every time.
17. **Your names and a short true bio.** May the site use your full names? Two or three true sentences each: how long you have lived and worked in Chattanooga, what each of you does day to day, and how long you have been in real estate or building. Any photos of you at work (on a job site, with a seller) you are happy to share?
18. **A place to meet.** Is there a real place you meet sellers and want public (an office, or a title company's conference room)? Or should the site say you come to the homeowner and keep the PO box as a mailing address only? (Google does not accept a PO box as a business address, and wary sellers look for a real one.) Also: what is the PO box's ZIP code? The site shows "PO Box 55, Hixson, TN" with no ZIP.
19. **Reply time and hours.** When do you answer the phone, and what reply time can you keep every time (for example "the same day")? Who gets the form alerts?
20. **Independent proof you may already have.** A BBB listing, a chamber membership, a title company or attorney willing to say a good word (with their OK), a past seller or buyer who would let us quote them. Anything that someone outside GLP can check.
21. **Which situations you really handle** (added after a cold read of the site). For each, "yes, often," "sometimes" or "no," and what you do: an inherited house (in probate, with several heirs, or an owner who lives out of state); an owner who is behind on the mortgage or has a foreclosure letter; a rental with a tenant, or one that is damaged; a house with liens or back taxes; a house with a lot of belongings left in it (do you take the contents, or does the seller clear it?). Who do you work with (an estate attorney, a title company)? How long does it usually take? We would write a short, plain answer only for the situations you really handle. *Why we ask: the first chips on Home are "Inherited homes" and "Financial stress," and the site says nothing on either yet.*
22. **The net sheet's default numbers.** Is 6% still what a Chattanooga agent charges (some now quote a little lower), and 2 to 3% for the seller's closing costs? Are you OK with counting about a week or two of the seller's house bills on the cash-sale side too (about a third of a month), so both sides are counted the same way? *Why we ask: three small defaults lean a little toward a cash sale, and the sheet is the page's main trust tool (review N27).*
23. **New homes wording and status.** Is the home "coming soon" or "now building" (the page says both)? When will it be finished? Are you OK softening "Ask about getting in before it's listed," which reads as pressure on a site that says "no pressure"? Are "Tired of crazy prices?" and "Anyone tired of crazy prices" your own words that you want to keep? Does the builder hold a license, or give a warranty?
