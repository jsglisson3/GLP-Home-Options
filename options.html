The seller carries the loan instead of a bank, and the buyer makes payments to the seller over time under agreed terms.

Straight answers.

The questions homeowners ask us most. Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

What does “as-is” mean?

It means you sell the home in the condition it's in today. If you sell directly to us, there are no repairs, no cleaning, and no showings. We just visit once so we can see the house.

Will I get less money selling directly than listing?

A direct offer is often lower than the best-case listing price, because you're trading some price for speed, certainty, and skipping repairs, showings, and months of carrying costs. Once commission, closing costs, and those costs come out, the gap can be smaller than it first looks. Run your own numbers on the net sheet.

How do I know which option is right for me?

Start with what matters most: speed, price, or staying put. Then talk to us. We'll go over the numbers together and tell you what we'd do in your position.

What are seller financing and a lease option?

With seller financing, the seller carries the loan instead of a bank, and the buyer pays the seller over time under agreed terms. With a lease option, a buyer rents the home with the right, but not the obligation, to buy it later at an agreed price. Terms vary, so have your own attorney or title company review any agreement before you sign.

Do I have to decide on the first call?

No. The first conversation is free, with no obligation. Take the time you need.
