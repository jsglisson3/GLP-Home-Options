How it works

A conversation, not a contract.

We start by talking. You stay in control of every step.

  1. 1

    Tell us your situation

    Share a few details about the home, your timing, and what's worrying you. In person or over the phone, whatever's easier.

  2. 2

    We lay out your options

    We go over the numbers together and show each realistic path with its pros, cons, and timeline. We'll tell you what we'd do in your position.

  3. 3

    You choose

    Take the time you need. Pick the path that fits your life, and we'll help with the next step if you want us to.
