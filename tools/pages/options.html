Case by case

Creative solutions. When the usual paths don't fit.

Not every situation fits a box. When the usual paths don't fit, we look at other options together, like seller financing and lease options.

Get my options

What these are, in plain English

Seller financing
The seller carries the loan instead of a bank, and the buyer makes payments to the seller over time under agreed terms.
Lease option
The buyer rents the home with the option to buy it later at an agreed price. They don't have to buy.
Something else
If your situation is unusual, tell us about it. We'll see what we can do.

Terms depend on the situation. You're always free to have your own attorney or title company review anything before you sign.

A good fit if

  • A regular sale doesn't solve your situation
  • You're open to something other than a regular sale
  • You'd like steady payments over time

Maybe not if

  • You want a clean, one-time payout now. A direct sale or a listing is simpler
