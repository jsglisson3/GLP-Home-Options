Case by case

Creative solutions. When the standard paths don't fit.

Not every situation fits a box. For unique situations where the standard paths don't feel like a fit, we explore structured options together, like seller financing and lease options.

Tell us about it

What these are, in plain English

Seller financing
Instead of the buyer getting a bank loan, the seller carries the loan, and the buyer makes payments over time under agreed terms.
Lease option
The buyer rents the home with the right, but not the obligation, to buy it later at an agreed price.
Something else
If your situation is unusual, tell us about it. We'll see what can be built.

Terms depend on the situation. You're always free to have your own attorney or title company review anything before you sign.

A good fit if

  • A standard sale doesn't solve your situation
  • You're open to a non-traditional deal
  • You'd like steady payments over time

Maybe not if

  • You want a clean, one-time payout now. A direct sale or a listing is simpler
