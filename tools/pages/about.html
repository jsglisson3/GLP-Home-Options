Why we do this

Homeowners hear two answers. There are more.

List it with an agent, or sell to an investor for less than the house is worth. That's what most people are told. But most people don't fit neatly into either box.

Inheritance, divorce, a job move, or just wanting life to be simpler: every situation is different, and each one deserves its own plan.

Our goal isn't to buy your house. It's to help you find the best path forward. Sometimes that means selling to us, sometimes it means listing, and sometimes it means keeping the home.
